Candles lit at the hollow shrine. One page to a winter. S. Ilva.

WINTER, FIRST YEAR.
Lit at the shrine: 1,104. Lit by me: 612. Lit by others who came: 492.
For the dead, as asked: 331. For the living, as asked: 161. For no reason anyone would give me: 112.
Wicks found still burning at first light: 7, and relit by whoever noticed, which I do not count as a loss.
Highest night: the bell's night. I did not count it. I could not see the page.

* * *

WINTER, TENTH YEAR.
Lit at the shrine: 4,930. Lit by me: 1,008. Lit by others who came: 3,922.
For the dead: 2,101. For the living: 1,400. For no reason anyone would give me: 421 — up every winter, and I have stopped asking why.

The count has never once gone down. Not one winter in ten. People come out of the dark and put a name to a wick, and the number only climbs. I have looked for a winter where it fell. There is not one.

That is the whole of my teaching, and I do not say it aloud: the coast is not short of asking.

— Ilva, at the candle-shrine.
