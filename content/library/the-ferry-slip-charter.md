Be it known to Saltreach, to the marsh, and to whoever stands wet at the far bank:

This slip was cut and the ferry set in it by the folk of the marsh, before there was a Guild to ask. The rule is plain, and it was plain first.

Any who can pole may carry any who cannot. A pole in a strong hand is a public thing, like a road or a well.

No coin passes when the tide is high. The water has already taken the crossing. It is not for sale twice.

* * *

The ferry is not the boat. The ferry is the carrying. Break the boat and a new one will plane out of the reeds in a season. Break the rule and the marsh is only water between two banks, and both banks are poorer.

The old folk add a line at the end of it, which is not charter and is kept anyway:

Nobody waits past deep-night alone. If there is a light at the slip, the crossing is open. If there is no light, shout. Somebody is awake.
