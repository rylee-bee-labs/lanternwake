Painted by the folk of Gullwatch, for whoever comes up from the beach with the tide at their heels.

THE CLIFF PATH — WALK IT AT LOW TIDE — NEVER AT DEEP-NIGHT.

The path is good ground. It is not good ground twice. The shingle moves. The last twenty paces are a ladder of roots and roots let go.

Come at the low water. Give yourself the whole of the ebb to be on it. At deep-night the path is a rumour and the drop below it is not.

* * *

Postscript, in smaller and cheerlier paint:

The lamp on the headland will be lit again one day. We paint it every spring whether it is true or not, because the paint keeps.

Until then, take a candle in a jar, come at the ebb, and walk early. There is soup at the ropewalk at this hour and the door is not shut.

Somebody will be glad to see you in.
