To the five towns of the coast, and to whoever keeps the road. The first lamp is failing, and I do not mean the Beacon. It is older than the Beacon. It has stood under the chapel since before any of our grandfathers, and it has gone down to an ember you could cover with a thumb. I have tried the wick. I have tried fresh oil. I have spoken to it the way you speak to a horse that will not rise, and it is not listening to me. I am going down to it tonight, alone, for there is no second lampwright to spare and no time left to go looking for one.

* * *

So I am asking you for a plain thing. While I am down there, hold the road lit yourselves. Not for the creed, for the cold. Candles in the windows, a hooded lantern on the causeway, the shrine burning the way it burns. The dark makes a name for itself in an unlit place, and then the name spreads. Hold your bit of road and it will hold you back. Hobb — do not quarrel with the reeve, do him the favour of the truth instead. Marta — keep the seat by the fire. If I come back I will knock twice. If I do not, it was not because I ran. Yours, Corwin Thale, Master Lampwright.
