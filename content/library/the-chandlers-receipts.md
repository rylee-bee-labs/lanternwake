Sold to the Guild of the Wake, on account: forty fathom hemp rope, tarred, for the mast of the Cliff Lamp. Two tuns lamp oil, Gullwatch pressed. One crate of wicks, cotton, forty-eight to the crate. A pint of tallow for the office candles. Carried up to the hall at Thornhollow, where the man with the cart signed for it in a mark I never could read and he never could be taught. Carriage paid. Nothing outstanding on this side of the page. The Guild was good for every penny, every year, without being chased for it once.

* * *

The last slip is different paper. Rope, oil and wicks as before, and beneath it, in a plain upright hand that every soul on this coast could pick out of a hundred: paid in full, and thank you for the light. Corwin Thale. I set the date beside it myself, for he was already out of the door and down the step. He paid standing up and he thanked you for the light. This book has sat behind my counter ten years and there is no other name written under his.
