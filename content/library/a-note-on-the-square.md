NOTICE — THORNHOLLOW SQUARE
Garrick Holt, Reeve.

Let every house keep a lantern lit after dusk, in a window or on a hook by the door, from this date forward and until the road stands open again.

Keep it hooded if you must. Keep it low if you must. Keep it.

Bring what oil you can spare to the reeve's stone. It will be measured out and written down, and nobody will be asked what they can afford.

The road is shut. The lamps do not have to be.

By order, the twelfth night of the first winter of the Dim.

* * *

The sheet itself: rain has washed most of the ink out of the top third, and the pin has been through it twice, in a different place, where the first hole gave way.

Under the order, in the same hand, a line added some months later and never struck out: "Lit: 41 houses. Lit: 44."

Nobody has taken it down. The grocer reads it every morning as he goes past and does not mention it. New people to the square read it, and then look along the street, counting windows.

It says until the road stands open again. The road is not open. The windows are lit.
