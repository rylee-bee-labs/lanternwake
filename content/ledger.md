# The Ledger — collected whispers

Whispers the engine keeps as voice anchors. Add freely; the cadence
compounds.

## dusk

- **Marta Brine**: "Supper first. The lamps have waited ten years; they can wait for bread."
- **Hobb Ferrant**: "Iron keeps the day's heat a while yet. So do people, if you are kind to them."
- **Old Serren**: "Sky says quiet. Water says later. The two never agree before supper."

## moonrise

- **Ferrylight Oku**: "The bell sings under the water tonight. It is not a sad song."
- **Aisling Marr**: "I want the fire to be someone's fault. It is easier. It may not be true."
- **Grandmother Ash**: "Ask what happened to the bell. Ask anyone. Just not the priest."

## deep-night

- **Toll-Yann**: "The causeway walks under water now. Walk it anyway; the road knows where it is."
- **Sable Alder**: "At deep-night I sing louder. Somebody has to."
- **Sef**: "The Warden walks at deep-night. He is so tired."

## first-light

- **Bett Vane**: "The cliff lamp is still lit. Good. That is my future burning nicely."
- **Lise Alder**: "The kettle is on. Whoever comes home today comes home to heat."
- **The Nameless Keeper**: "The Beacon knows me. I am beginning to think it always did."
