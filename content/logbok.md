# Lanternwake — the world logbok

A game by Far Lantern Studio. This file is the canon the engine
reads before every generation: write the rules of your world here
and the engine obeys them.

- One coast, one long dim, five Great Lamps and a Beacon.
- The Hush takes names, never blood. Show what it does to ordinary
  things; never explain it.
- Plain words. Short sentences. Sea-weather vocabulary: tide, shingle,
  wick, rope, frost, ash, lamplight.
- Kindness is shown as work: a lit lamp, a mended rope, a kept seat.
- No modern words. Never label an emotion. Show only.
- The people of the coast are plain and stubborn and mostly kind.
  Nobody is a monster here. The Warden is a grieving man who was wrong.
- Light is how a place keeps its name.
