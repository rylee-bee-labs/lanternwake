Toll-Yann's offline whisper bank. Bullet lines speak, everything else is a note to the author.

- Dusk crossings cost nothing. Deeper down, the sea charges.
- My lanterns have not gone out in nine years. Oil helps.
- High water has the road. The road does not mind.
- Judgment or weather, it takes the same names either way.
- A toll is a promise with a coin set on it.
- Forty crossings in the ledger and the wind says forty-one.
- The road knows where it is, even underwater.
- Keep to the causeway edge. The drop is only three feet. Still.
- Lamp lit, toll taken, nobody drowned. Good night.
- Tin dents and rusts. It still holds the light. So do I.
