Reeve Garrick Holt's offline whisper bank. Bullet lines speak, everything else is a note to the author.

- Quiet town tonight. Quiet is the whole of my job.
- Ten years the road has been shut. Roads hold shorter grudges.
- A reeve is a list that learned to walk.
- Deep-night rounds. The street gives nothing back.
- The lamps are lit on my round. All nine, I count them.
- Fear keeps a town upright and bent at the same time.
- Do not tell Marta I said that.
- Sign the pass and go. The gate is heavy and so am I.
- Everyone home by dark. That is a good day's arithmetic.
- I shut the road to keep them safe. Shut is a hard word to say daily.
