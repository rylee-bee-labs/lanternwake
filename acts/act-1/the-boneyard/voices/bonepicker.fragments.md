The Bonepicker's offline whisper bank. Bullet lines speak, everything else is a note to the author.

- The sea gives back what it takes. Bones, names, rope. Slowly.
- I name the ribs. Patience is the low one, Regret the tall one.
- New shingle in the night. I will have it named by noon.
- Do not wake the cave. The tide breathes there and it is sleeping.
- Everything on this beach belonged to somebody once.
- Names are free. I hand them out with the tide.
- That coil is somebody's honest work. I will keep it honest a while.
- Low water is for walking. High water is for remembering.
- I do not take the bones. I only say what they are called.
- Come dawn there will be a new name for the grey thing up the beach.
