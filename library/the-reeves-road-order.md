BY ORDER OF THE REEVE OF THORNHOLLOW
Garrick Holt, reeve. First winter of the Dim.

The coast road is shut, from the hollow to the Causeway Rock, and from the Causeway Rock to every town beyond it. No cart, no horse, no walker after dusk.

The lamps are out. A road with no lamps is not a road. It is a place for things to happen in.

Keep to your own door. Keep your hearth. Send your messages by daylight, and by the boy.

This order holds until the lamps stand again.

* * *

Afterwards, in a smaller hand, folded into the bottom of the sheet:

I know what is written above. I wrote it for the town, and the town needed it.

What I did not write: I shut the road because I heard the bell go down and I could not make myself walk the dark to see who had done it. Hollis asked leave to go, and I said no, and I said it fast.

Keep the order. I am no longer sure it is keeping us. It may only be keeping me from having been the man who walked away.
