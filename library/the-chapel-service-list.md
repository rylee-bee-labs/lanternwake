Services at Gullwatch Chapel — one line a feast, kept by the priest's hand.

First year of the Dim: Candlemas, forty-one souls. Ash Wednesday, rain, full. Palm Sunday, fronds cut in the Weald, full. Midsummer light-round, the lamp walked the cliff and back with eleven behind it.

Second year: Candlemas, thirty-eight. Ash Wednesday, thirty. The light-round kept.

Third year: Candlemas, thirty. That October the bell rang for the equinox storm and went into the water with the roof timbers.

Fourth year: Candlemas, twenty-four. Ash Wednesday, nineteen. No bell.

* * *

Fifth year: Candlemas, twenty-two. The bell is not rung and is not spoken of.

Sixth: nineteen. No light-round; the cliff lamp was out.

Seventh: fourteen. Small feasts only.

Eighth: seven. The line for Candlemas was begun twice and finished once.

no bell

Ninth: the ink of that line is darker than the rest. Someone wrote it in a hurry, or hard.

Tenth: Candlemas, eleven souls. Nothing written after them. The list ends with the page still ruled for years that have not come.
