THE GUILD, REBUILT — plan of Vane, Bett, of Gullwatch. Lamp by lamp. Nobody has asked me. That is not an obstacle, that is the start of every list.

One. Learn every lamp on the coast by walking to it: all ninety-nine standing, and where each one stood when it fell.
Two. Oil. How much, how often, in frost and in a wet spring. Ask Aunt Orra. Do not ask twice.
Three. Wick-splice, glass-mending, and the storm-lantern hinge that Hobb does and will not explain.
Four. The Evercan. Whoever has it, find them, and do not be shy about it.
Five. Read the old Guild books until I can write new ones.

* * *

Six. Teach it. One child at a time, at the hearth, with a lamp to work on. The Guild was never a building. It was people who knew things and told other people.

Seven. Paperwork. Quill is right. Paper first.

Eight. — struck out on the second night, hard:

be patient

I have crossed it out since and I will cross it out again. Patience is what people say when they want the road to stay shut. The road does not want patience. It wants wicks.

— Bett, the corner house, deep-night, hands filthy, page torn out of the back of the chandler's book.
