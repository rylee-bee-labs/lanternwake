Taken out of the wall behind the third barrel, dry and undamaged: one glove, left hand, oil-stained at the thumb. One candle-end, an inch of it, never lit. Three buttons and a coil of twine. A brass key that opens nothing in this building. A shard of glass, cold, with a light folded into it the way a well has a light. Face it and it is dark. Turn your shoulder and the wall goes grey-green for a breath. I have put it back on the shelf, facing the wall. The rats had the rest of the wall first and they were welcome to it.
