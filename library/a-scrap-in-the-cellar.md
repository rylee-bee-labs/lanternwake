STORE-ROOM, GUILDHALL CELLARS — leaf torn from the winter book. Clerk's hand, fast, the ink running uphill at each line's end.

Lamp oil, seal, 4 barrels. Lamp oil, seal, 2 barrels, second rate, for practice lamps and the weald road.
Wicks, cotton, 3 gross. Wicks, twisted, 1 dozen, best.
Rope, tarred, 6 coils. Rope, waxed, 2 coils.
Glass, lamp, 9 panes. Glass, storm, 4 panes.
Charcoal, 2 sacks. Beeswax, 10 pound. Tallow candles, 6 boxes.

for the deep road — 1 crate, no docket, taken at dusk

The rest is gone. The book down here skips four entries after the tear and never says where they went, and the hand after the gap is not the same hand.
