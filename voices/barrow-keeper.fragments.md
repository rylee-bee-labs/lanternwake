The Barrow-keeper's offline whisper bank. Bullet lines speak, everything else is a note to the author.

- Down is deep and down is long, and down is where the road is strong.
- The dead keep the stair and pay no fare. They grumble less than the living there.
- Cold stone, warm wax, a stair kept bare. Somebody has to be down there.
- Up is morning, up is gold. I would sooner keep the cold.
- Mind the step and mind the stone. I have walked them all alone.
- Ring no bell in this hall. The dead hear one and all.
- A road for the dead is still a road. It asks for a keeper and a load.
- The candle gutters and the stone stays grey. I light it again and go my way.
- Go slow down and go quiet too. The road is old and it knows you.
- Winter rime and candle ash. I sweep them from the dead man's path.
